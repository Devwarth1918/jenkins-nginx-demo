20 August – 24 August

A journey through the Himalayas, sacred temples, breathtaking valleys, and unforgettable memories.

Explore Trip

📍 Travel Route

🚗 Meerut

Starting point of our unforgettable Himalayan road trip.

🕉 Rishikesh

The Yoga Capital of the World and gateway to the Himalayas.

🏔 Madhyamaheshwar

One of the sacred Panch Kedar temples dedicated to Lord Shiva.

🙏 Kalpeshwar

The only Panch Kedar temple open throughout the year.

❄ Auli

India's famous hill station known for snow, ropeways, and Himalayan views.

🗓 Day-wise Itinerary

📅 20 August

Departure from Meerut early morning and arrival at Rishikesh. Explore Triveni Ghat, Laxman Jhula, Ram Jhula, and enjoy the evening Ganga Aarti.

📅 21 August

Journey towards Madhyamaheshwar. Experience scenic mountain roads, rivers, forests, and begin the trek.

📅 22 August

Darshan at Madhyamaheshwar Temple. Continue the journey towards Kalpeshwar and explore the beautiful Urgam Valley.

📅 23 August

Visit Kalpeshwar Temple and then drive towards Auli. Enjoy ropeway rides and panoramic Himalayan landscapes.

📅 24 August

Morning sightseeing in Auli followed by the return journey with unforgettable memories.
