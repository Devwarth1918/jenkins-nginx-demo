🕉 The Legend of Lord Shri Ram

From Birth to the Victory of Dharma

1. Birth of Shri Ram

Lord Shri Ram was born in Ayodhya to King Dasharatha and Queen Kaushalya. His birth is celebrated every year as Ram Navami.

2. Education under Guru Vashistha

Ram received education in scriptures, warfare, leadership, ethics, and the responsibilities of an ideal ruler under Sage Vashistha.

3. Journey with Sage Vishwamitra

Ram protected sages from demons like Tataka and Subahu and gained divine celestial weapons.

4. Sita Swayamvar

Lord Ram broke the mighty bow of Lord Shiva and married Goddess Sita.

5. Exile (Vanvas)

Due to Queen Kaikeyi's two boons, Ram willingly accepted fourteen years of exile. Sita and Lakshman accompanied him.

6. Panchavati & Golden Deer

The appearance of the golden deer led to Ravana abducting Sita, changing the course of history.

7. Hanuman Meets Shri Ram

Hanuman became Ram's greatest devotee and messenger. Their friendship became a symbol of devotion and loyalty.

8. Ram Setu

The Vanara Sena constructed the Ram Setu across the ocean to reach Lanka.

9. Battle of Lanka

A fierce battle took place between the forces of Dharma and Adharma. Many great warriors participated.

10. Ravana's Defeat

Lord Ram defeated Ravana using the Brahmastra, symbolizing the triumph of righteousness over evil.

11. Return to Ayodhya

After completing the exile, Lord Ram, Sita, and Lakshman returned to Ayodhya. The people celebrated with lamps, giving rise to the festival of Deepavali.

12. Ram Rajya

Lord Ram was crowned King of Ayodhya. His reign became known as Ram Rajya, remembered as an ideal kingdom based on justice, compassion, and righteousness.

॥ जय श्री राम ॥

"The victory of Dharma is eternal."
